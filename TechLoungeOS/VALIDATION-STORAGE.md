## Rules for subsequent tests

- Keep one active builder VM with its required backing chain, one current workspace, one candidate export and one known-good fallback. Keep logs/screenshots/reports, not every historical full disk image.
- Check C: and D: actual free space before every large allocation. Estimate peak build/USB/install space together. Stop new allocations if the reserve is insufficient.
- Aim for at most 300 GiB under the D: validation root and at least 100 GiB free host space before additional large tests. Reclaim completed disposable work before creating another full VM.
- Reuse the main read-only payload disc. Change small script-only media separately instead of duplicating full tools/Office/Tweaks/update payloads for each probe.
- Never delete a live image, any backing image, a host-mounted image or a linked path. Do not follow directory reparse points during inventory/cleanup.
- Never reset/kill a VM during servicing or writing to reclaim space. Finish the operation and shut down normally before removing its disposable files.

`Clear-ObsoleteValidation.ps1` previews a reviewed CSV plan by default. Applying requires an administrator, a successful host mount query and repeated path/attribute/in-use checks. It never uses forced deletion. Its recorded plan is historical after this cleanup; it is not an unattended sweeper or permission to delete new artifacts.
